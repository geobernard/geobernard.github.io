Our Approach

NEXUS Collaborative addresses complex humanitarian search and recovery challenges through an interdisciplinary, evidence-based approach that integrates expertise from the humanities, social sciences, and technological fields. We combine historical research, geospatial science, anthropology, advanced sensing technologies, and artificial intelligence to identify, analyze, and interpret hidden evidence in landscapes affected by war, sectarian conflict, and natural disasters.

Our work begins by bringing together diverse sources of information—including historical records, archival materials, witness accounts, remote sensing data, and geospatial analyses—to develop a comprehensive understanding of each search area. We then apply innovative technologies and scientific methods to detect, evaluate, and prioritize locations that may contain evidence of missing persons, unmarked burials, downed aircraft, unexploded ordnance, or historic battlefields.

Beyond individual investigations, we are committed to advancing the science of humanitarian search and recovery. We develop and evaluate new search technologies, establish evidence-based recovery protocols, and train the next generation of researchers and practitioners. Through collaboration with academic institutions, government agencies, and humanitarian organizations, we translate research into practical solutions that improve recovery efforts, strengthen forensic investigations, and preserve our shared historical and cultural heritage.
