Locating and Recovering the Missing

Unmarked burials represent some of the most challenging and sensitive contexts in humanitarian recovery and forensic investigation. NEXUS Collaborative conducts interdisciplinary research to develop improved methods for locating, documenting, and understanding burial sites that are concealed, disturbed, or no longer visible in the landscape. Our work combines archaeological methods, forensic anthropology, geospatial science, remote sensing, advanced sensing technologies, and artificial intelligence to identify evidence and guide ethical recovery efforts.

By integrating historical records, landscape analysis, field investigations, and emerging technologies, we seek to improve the detection and interpretation of potential burial locations while minimizing disturbance to affected landscapes and communities. Our research focuses on developing scientifically rigorous approaches that support the recovery and identification of missing individuals, strengthen forensic investigations, and provide answers to families and communities affected by conflict, violence, and disasters.

NEXUS is committed to advancing respectful and evidence-based practices for the recovery of human remains. Through interdisciplinary collaboration and the development of innovative tools and protocols, we contribute to humanitarian efforts that restore dignity, preserve memory, and support the search for the missing.
