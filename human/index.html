Locating and Recovering the Missing

We are a team of scholars dedicated to transforming hidden information into actionable knowledge that uncovers concealed evidence of missing persons in areas affected by war, sectarian conflict, and disasters.

We combine historical research, geospatial science, advanced sensing technologies, anthropology and AI to advance humanitarian recovery missions, forensic investigations, and archaeological discovery.

Our research themes include:

  • Unmarked Burials

  • Downed Aircraft

  • Unexploded Ordnance

  • Historic Battlefields

  • Development and Integration of Human Search Technologies

  • Development and Implementation of Protocols for the Recovery of Human Remains
