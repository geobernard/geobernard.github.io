Support Our Mission

Advancing Humanitarian Research

Your support helps NEXUS Collaborative advance innovative research that transforms hidden information into actionable knowledge for humanitarian recovery, forensic investigations, and archaeological discovery. Contributions enable our team to develop and apply new technologies, conduct field investigations, support student research opportunities, and advance interdisciplinary approaches for locating and recovering evidence of missing persons and past human activity.

By supporting NEXUS, you help strengthen research at the intersection of anthropology, archaeology, geospatial science, remote sensing, artificial intelligence, engineering, and advanced sensing technologies. Your generosity allows us to pursue solutions to complex challenges, train the next generation of researchers, and develop tools and methods that can make a meaningful difference for communities affected by conflict, disasters, and unresolved historical events.

Every contribution supports our mission to reveal hidden evidence, preserve human stories, and advance scientific approaches that serve humanity. Together, we can improve the search for the missing, support recovery efforts, and build a deeper understanding of the landscapes and events that shape our shared history.

Your support can help fund:

  • Undergraduate and graduate research opportunities
  • Field investigations and data collection
  • Drone, sensing, and geospatial technologies
  • Research software and computational resources
  • Development of humanitarian search and recovery methods

To discuss project-specific donation opportunities, please contact Dr. Stephen Mihm.
