Undergraduate Research Assistant

Drone-Based Remote Sensing and AI

NEXUS Collaborative is seeking an undergraduate research assistant to support the development and implementation of drone-based artificial intelligence technologies for humanitarian search and recovery applications. The student will assist with drone data collection, image and geospatial data processing, AI-based analysis workflows, and evaluation of methods designed to identify and interpret hidden evidence in complex landscapes. This opportunity provides hands-on experience at the intersection of unmanned aerial systems, artificial intelligence, remote sensing, and geospatial science while contributing to interdisciplinary research that advances humanitarian recovery, forensic investigations, and archaeological discovery.

Students from fields such as computer science, engineering, geography, geospatial science, environmental science or related disciplines are encouraged to apply. Experience with programming and AI is required.

If interested, email cv to Dr. Sergio Bernardes (sbernard@uga.edu).
